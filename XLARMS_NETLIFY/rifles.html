Frequently asked questions — Rifles

Do rifles ship to my home? No. Rifles ship only to a licensed FFL dealer of your choice for background check and pickup.

What is the difference between 5.56 and .223? They are similar but not identical chamberings — always check your rifle's barrel marking and use the ammunition it is chambered for. Never substitute calibers.

Do you carry AR-15 parts and uppers? Yes — see Upper Receivers & Conversion Kits in the shop, alongside complete rifles.
