Frequently asked questions — Accessories

Do accessories require an FFL? No. Accessories ship normally, subject to state restrictions — magazines and other items can be restricted in some states, and those restrictions block drop shipment at checkout.

How do I find a holster that fits my pistol? Search by brand + model and check the product's fit field. A holster made for a Glock 17 will not reliably fit a Glock 19, even though the brands match.

Do you carry magazines for my firearm? Magazines are listed under Magazines and High Capacity Magazines in the shop — filter by brand and model, and check capacity against your state's rules.
