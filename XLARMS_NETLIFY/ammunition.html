Frequently asked questions — Ammunition

Does ammunition require an FFL? No. Ammunition ships normally, but state and local restrictions still apply at checkout and some destinations are blocked.

Can I buy ammunition in bulk? Yes — many listings show units per box and per case in the product details. Filter by caliber and check stock before ordering.

Is 9mm the same as 9x19? Yes — 9mm Luger, 9x19 and 9mm Parabellum are the same cartridge. The shop canonicalises those spellings so a search for any of them finds the same products.
