Frequently asked questions — Optics & Sights

Do optics require an FFL? No. Optics and sights ship normally, with no FFL transfer required.

What magnification do I need? For most AR-15 and hunting use, a 1-6x or 3-9x scope covers the common ranges; red dots suit close-range speed. Match the optic to your shooting distance, not just the price.
