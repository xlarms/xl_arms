Frequently asked questions — Shotguns

What gauge should I choose? 12 gauge is the most versatile all-round choice; 20 gauge recoils less and suits smaller-framed shooters; 410 bore is light-recoiling but has less payload. Match ammunition gauge to your shotgun's marking.

Do shotguns ship to my home? No. Shotguns ship only to a licensed FFL dealer for background check and pickup.

Do you sell shotgun ammunition? Yes — see Ammunition, filtered by 12, 20, 28 gauge or 410 bore.

FFL notice: Firearms ship to your FFL, not to your home. Ammunition and accessories ship normally, subject to state restrictions. You must be 18 or older to enter this site.
