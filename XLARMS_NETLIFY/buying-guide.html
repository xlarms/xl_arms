Buying a Gun Online — How It Works

Buying a firearm online is straightforward once you know the one rule that matters: a firearm never ships to your home. It ships to a licensed FFL dealer you choose, and you complete the background check and pickup there.

Step by step

  1. Choose your firearm in the shop and check the in-stock count.
  2. Choose your FFL dealer at checkout. If your dealer is not listed, contact us and we will help add them.
  3. We ship to that dealer, not to your home. Ammunition and accessories ship normally, subject to state restrictions.
  4. Complete the background check at the dealer and pick up your firearm there. You must be 18 or older to enter this site, and 21 or older to purchase a handgun from a dealer under federal law.

Price-match & FFL fee offer

We sell at MAP. If you find a price lower elsewhere, ask about our price-match offer. If you buy a firearm, we cover your FFL transfer fee up to $25 if proven.

Pickup Timing

Orders placed Monday through Thursday will be ready for pickup in 24–48 hours. Orders placed Friday, Saturday, or Sunday are treated as Monday orders.
