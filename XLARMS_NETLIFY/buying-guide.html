Buying a Gun Online — How It Works

XL Arms is no longer a walk-in store. All sales are online or by phone, shipped from the Louisiana warehouse. A firearm never ships to your home — it ships to a licensed FFL dealer you choose, or to our Norcross office for pickup after your background check is approved. Please do not just show up at an address.

Step by step

  1. Order online or by phone — choose your firearm in the shop and check the in-stock count, or call (678) 691-6375.
  2. Choose how you will receive it: ship to a licensed FFL dealer near you, or pick up at our Norcross office.
  3. For office pickup, send your paperwork first: send your completed Form 4473 and your Georgia ID/license, so the background check can be run before you travel — so you don't make a trip only to be delayed or denied.
  4. Once approved, pick up within 24–48 hours at our office, or complete the background check and pickup at your chosen FFL dealer if your firearm was shipped there. You must be 18 or older to enter this site, and 21 or older to purchase a handgun from a dealer under federal law.
  5. Ammunition and accessories ship normally, subject to state restrictions.

Price-match & FFL fee offer

We sell at MAP/MSRP. If you find a price lower elsewhere, send us the link to that offer and ask about our price-match. If you buy a firearm, we cover your FFL transfer fee up to $25 if proven — send us a link to the dealer's listing, or their contact information from Google, so we can contact the FFL to confirm, and we will send the reimbursement via Zelle or Cash App. With no storefront overhead, we keep prices as low as possible — and we will beat or match almost anyone's price when possible.

Pickup Timing

Orders placed Monday through Thursday will be ready for pickup in 24–48 hours. Orders placed Friday, Saturday, or Sunday are treated as Monday orders. For office pickup, the 24–48 hours runs from background-check approval.
