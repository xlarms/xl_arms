Why buy from XL Arms?

XL Arms is a licensed firearms dealer in Norcross, Georgia, serving Metro Atlanta and shipping nationwide to licensed dealers. Our catalog is synced from RSR, one of the largest firearms distributors in the U.S., so the stock counts and MAP prices you see are real — not a static list that went stale months ago. If a price is lower elsewhere, ask about our price-match offer. If you buy a firearm, we cover your FFL transfer fee up to $25 if proven.

How buying a firearm online works

Choose your firearm, check out, and choose a licensed FFL dealer near you for delivery. We ship the firearm to that dealer — never directly to your home — and you complete the background check and pickup there. Ammunition and accessories ship normally, subject to state shipping restrictions at checkout. You must be 18 or older to enter this site, and 21 or older to purchase a handgun from a dealer under federal law.
