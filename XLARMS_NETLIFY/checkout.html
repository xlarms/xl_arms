Checkout — Choose One

Choose how you want to receive your firearm. You must choose one — the background check, the fee, and the payment all follow the option you pick. Firearms are never shipped to your home address.

Option 1 — Norcross Office Pickup

  • Send your pickup information ahead through the intake page — no Adobe, no printing, no scanning
  • $15 background-check service fee (not a deposit), paid upfront for the pre-NICS service
  • If you pass and purchase the gun, the $15 is credited toward the price — you pay only the balance at pickup (example: $539 gun, $15 upfront, $524 due at pickup)
  • If you are denied, including a delayed check that later comes back denied, the $15 service fee is kept, for the service performed
  • Once you have paid, there is no cancellation — the check runs immediately
  • If XL Arms cannot deliver the gun, the $15 is refunded
  • You pay on pickup, not the full price online — the balance only, after the $15 credit
  • Bring your original Georgia ID/license and sign the official Form 4473 again in person at pickup

Choose pickup — send your pickup information →

The $15 service fee is collected once the payment route is approved — the exact payment step will be confirmed with you by phone before you are charged. Until then, call (678) 691-6375 to arrange pickup.

Option 2 — Ship to Your FFL Dealer

  • Choose a licensed FFL dealer near you to receive the firearm — Find a dealer near you →
  • No $15 fee, no pre-NICS from XL Arms — your receiving FFL handles the background check
  • Pay the full price online once online checkout is live (payment gateway to be confirmed)
  • Complete the Form 4473 and background check at your FFL's counter before taking possession
