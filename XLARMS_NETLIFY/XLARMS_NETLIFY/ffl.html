FFL Dealers & Transfers

An FFL is a Federal Firearms License holder — a licensed dealer who can legally receive a firearm and transfer it to you after a background check.

How a transfer works

Choose a dealer near you at checkout and we ship your firearm to them. The dealer runs the background check, you complete the paperwork, and you pick up your firearm from the dealer. Transfer fees are set by the dealer — XL Arms covers your FFL transfer fee up to $25 if proven.

Do ammunition and accessories need an FFL?

No. Ammunition and accessories ship normally, subject to state and local shipping restrictions at checkout.
