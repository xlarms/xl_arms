How buying a firearm online works

Choose your firearm, check out, and choose a licensed FFL dealer near you for delivery. We ship the firearm to that dealer — never directly to your home — and you complete the background check and pickup there. Ammunition and accessories ship normally, subject to state shipping restrictions at checkout. You must be 18 or older to enter this site, and 21 or older to purchase a handgun from a dealer under federal law.

Frequently asked questions

Do you ship firearms to my home? No. Firearms ship only to a licensed FFL dealer of your choice for background check and pickup.

Are your prices MAP? Yes. We sell at MAP (minimum advertised price), with a price-match offer and up to $25 of your FFL transfer fee covered if proven.

Do you have ammunition in stock? Stock is updated regularly. Use the shop's “In stock only” filter to see only what is available right now.

Pickup timing: Orders placed Monday through Thursday will be ready for pickup in 24–48 hours. Orders placed Friday, Saturday, or Sunday are treated as Monday orders.
