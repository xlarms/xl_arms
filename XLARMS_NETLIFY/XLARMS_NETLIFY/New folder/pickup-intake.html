The information below is the same information the official Form 4473 asks for, so our staff can prepare your pickup — but it does NOT replace the official form. You will bring your original Georgia ID/license and sign the official Form 4473 again in person at pickup. That step is required.
  3. $15 background-check service fee. Paid upfront for the background-check service. If you pass and purchase the gun, the $15 is credited toward the price — you pay only the balance at pickup. If you are denied, including a delayed check that later comes back denied, the $15 service fee is kept, for the service performed. Once you have paid, there is no cancellation — the check runs immediately. If XL Arms cannot deliver the gun, the $15 is refunded. This is a service fee, not a deposit.

These are the standard Form 4473 questions. Answer truthfully — our staff will go through them with you again on the official form at pickup.
